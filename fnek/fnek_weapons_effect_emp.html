NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Weapons Effect: Electromagnetic Pulse (EMP)

Electromagnetic Pulse (EMP) is produced by the interaction of prompt gamma rays with the atmosphere, generating a large, rapid electric field pulse.

A high-altitude nuclear detonation (HEMP, >30 km) can produce an EMP covering continental distances, potentially disabling unshielded electronics, power grids, communications, and electronic systems over thousands of square kilometers. Does not directly harm personnel but can cause catastrophic infrastructure failure.
