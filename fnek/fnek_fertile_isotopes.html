Common Fertile Isotopes

  • U-238: Captures a neutron to become U-239, which beta-decays to Np-239, then to Pu-239 (fissile).
  • Th-232: Captures a neutron to become Th-233, which beta-decays to Pa-233, then to U-233 (fissile).

Fertile nuclides cannot sustain fission themselves but breed fissile material via neutron capture.
