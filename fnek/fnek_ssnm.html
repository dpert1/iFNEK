NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Strategic Special Nuclear Material (SSNM)

Strategic Special Nuclear Material (SSNM):

  • Highly Enriched Uranium (HEU): U-235 enriched to ≥20%; weapons-grade is ≥90%.
  • U-233: Bred from Th-232; fissile and weapons-usable.
  • Plutonium (any isotopic composition): Primarily Pu-239; produced in reactors from U-238.

SSNM is subject to strict safeguards under the IAEA and U.S. law due to its direct use potential in nuclear weapons.
