External Gamma-Ray Absorbed Dose Rate

\[\dot{D} = \phi E_\gamma \left(\frac{\mu_{en}}{\rho}\right)\]

  • \(\dot{D}\): absorbed dose rate [Gy/s]
  • \(\phi\): photon flux [#/cm²-s]
  • \(E_\gamma\): photon energy [MeV]
  • \(\mu_{en}/\rho\): mass energy-absorption coefficient [cm²/g]
