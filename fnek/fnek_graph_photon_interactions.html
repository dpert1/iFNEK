Figure 5: Photon Interaction Dominance by Energy and Z

Shape:

Plot of absorber Z vs. photon energy (hν in MeV). Three regions separated by two curves: photoelectric dominant (low energy/high Z), Compton dominant (middle region), pair production dominant (high energy/high Z).

Significance:

  • Shows which interaction mechanism dominates for a given photon energy and shield material.
  • For medical/industrial gamma energies (0.1–1 MeV) in tissue (Z ~ 7): Compton scatter dominates.
  • For high-Z shields (lead, Z=82) at low energies: photoelectric dominates — explains lead's effectiveness at diagnostic X-ray energies.
  • At very high energies in high-Z material: pair production dominates.
  • Used to select appropriate shielding material for a given radiation source.
