Gamma Interaction: Pair Production

The incident photon is converted into an electron-positron pair in the Coulomb field of a nucleus. The photon must supply at least the rest mass energy of both particles.

  • Threshold: \(E_\gamma \geq 2 m_e c^2 = 1.022 \ \text{MeV}\)
  • Dominant at high energies (>5–10 MeV)
  • Cross section scales as \(Z^2\)
  • Positron subsequently annihilates with an electron, producing two 0.511 MeV photons
