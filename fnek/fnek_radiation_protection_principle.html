NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Basic Principles of Radiation Protection

The three fundamental methods of radiation protection:

  1. Time: Minimize time spent near a radiation source — dose is directly proportional to exposure time.
  2. Distance: Maximize distance from the source — dose rate from a point source decreases with the inverse square of distance (\(\dot{D} \propto 1/r^2\)).
  3. Shielding: Interpose appropriate shielding material — attenuates radiation exponentially (\(I = I_0 e^{-\mu x}\)).
