Charged Particle Interaction: Excitation

Excitation occurs when a charged particle transfers energy to a bound electron, raising it to a higher energy state without ejecting it.

The excited atom subsequently returns to ground state by emitting a photon (fluorescence) or transferring energy to neighboring atoms (non-radiative relaxation). Excitation contributes to energy loss alongside ionization but produces no free ion pairs.
