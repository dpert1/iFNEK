NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Weapons Effect: Thermal Radiation

Thermal radiation accounts for approximately 35% of a nuclear weapon's total energy.

The fireball radiates intense heat and light across a wide area. Effects include flash blindness, skin burns (first, second, third degree), and ignition of flammable materials. Thermal fluence [cal/cm²] determines burn severity. Thermal effects have a larger range than blast for multi-megaton weapons and can cause mass fires (firestorm) in urban areas.
