Reactor Stable Period After Large Negative Reactivity

Stable period after insertion of large negative reactivity: \(T \approx 85 \ \text{s}\)

After a large negative reactivity insertion (e.g., scram), the reactor power does not drop instantly. Prompt neutrons die out quickly, but the longest-lived delayed neutron precursor group (half-life ~55 s) governs the asymptotic decay. The reactor power falls with a stable period of approximately 85 seconds.
