Gamma Interaction: Photoelectric Effect

The incident photon is completely absorbed by a bound inner-shell electron, which is ejected as a photoelectron with kinetic energy \(T = E_\gamma - E_b\).

  • Dominant at low photon energies (<0.1 MeV)
  • Cross section scales as \(\sigma_{pe} \propto Z^{4-5} / E_\gamma^{3.5}\)
  • Leaves a vacancy filled by characteristic X-ray or Auger electron emission
