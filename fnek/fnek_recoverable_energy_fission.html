Recoverable Energy per Fission

\[E_R \approx 200 \ \text{MeV}\]

The recoverable (deposited) energy per fission event of U-235. Includes kinetic energy of fission fragments (~168 MeV), prompt gammas, betas, and delayed radiation. Excludes energy carried away by neutrinos (~10 MeV).
