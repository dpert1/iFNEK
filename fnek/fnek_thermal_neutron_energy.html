Thermal Neutron Energy

\[E_{th} = 0.0253 \ \text{eV} \quad (T = 20^\circ\text{C})\]

The most probable energy of a neutron in thermal equilibrium with its surroundings at room temperature. Corresponds to a neutron speed of ~2200 m/s. Cross sections are often tabulated at this energy.
