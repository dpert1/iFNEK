NE 300 — Fundamentals of Nuclear Engineering

Multiplication Factor (k)

\[k = \frac{\text{neutrons in generation } (n+1)}{\text{neutrons in generation } (n)}\]

  • \(k < 1\): subcritical — chain reaction dies out.
  • \(k = 1\): critical — self-sustaining chain reaction.
  • \(k > 1\): supercritical — chain reaction grows.
