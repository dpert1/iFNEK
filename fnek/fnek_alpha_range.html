NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Range of Alpha Particles in Air

Range of alpha particle (<6 MeV) in air: <5 cm

Alpha particles are doubly charged and heavy, causing dense ionization that rapidly depletes their energy. They are stopped by a few centimeters of air, a sheet of paper, or the dead outer layer of skin. Alpha emitters are not an external hazard but are highly dangerous if inhaled or ingested.
