NE 452 — Instrumentation and Shielding

Good Neutron Shielding Properties

  • Low atomic mass number (Z/A): Light nuclei (especially hydrogen in water or polyethylene) transfer the most energy per elastic collision, efficiently moderating fast neutrons to thermal energies.
  • High neutron absorption cross section: Once thermalized, neutrons must be absorbed. Materials like boron (B-10, σ_a ~ 3840 b) and cadmium are common thermal neutron absorbers incorporated into shields.
