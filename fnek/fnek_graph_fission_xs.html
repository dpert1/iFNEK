Figure 4: Microscopic Fission Cross Section for U-235

Shape:

Log-log plot. Three regions: (1) 1/v region at low energy where σ ∝ 1/E^½, (2) resonance region (1 eV – 1 keV) with sharp peaks, (3) smooth continuum at high energies (>1 keV).

Significance:

  • Very high cross section at thermal energies (~580 b at 0.0253 eV) — explains why thermal reactors are efficient.
  • The 1/v region means slower neutrons are more likely to cause fission — the basis of moderation.
  • Resonance region represents specific nuclear energy levels; neutrons at these energies are strongly absorbed (resonance escape probability p addresses this).
  • Cross section drops significantly at fast energies — fast reactors require higher enrichment to compensate.
