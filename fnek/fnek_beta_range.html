NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Range of Beta Particles in Air

Range of beta particle in air: ~12 ft per MeV

Beta particles travel much farther than alphas due to their lower charge and mass. A 1 MeV beta travels ~12 ft (~3.7 m) in air. They can penetrate skin and cause burns but are stopped by a few mm of plastic, aluminum, or glass. Bremsstrahlung X-rays produced in high-Z shielding materials (e.g., lead) can be a secondary hazard.
