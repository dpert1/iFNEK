NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Supercritical Assembly: Gun-Type

Gun-type assembly uses U-235 (HEU).

A subcritical mass of HEU is propelled down a gun barrel into a second subcritical target mass, forming a supercritical combined mass upon impact. Simple and reliable but inefficient — requires large amounts of HEU and is too slow for Pu-239 (pre-detonation risk due to Pu-240 spontaneous fission). Used in the Little Boy weapon.
