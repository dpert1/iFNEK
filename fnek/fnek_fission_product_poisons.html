NE 355 — Nuclear Reactor Engineering

Common Fission Product Poisons

  • Xe-135: Largest neutron poison in thermal reactors (\(\sigma_a \approx 2.6 \times 10^6\) b). Builds up over ~40–50 hours; causes "xenon oscillations" and iodine pit after shutdown.
  • Sm-149: Second largest poison (\(\sigma_a \approx 4 \times 10^4\) b). Reaches equilibrium over several days; increases after shutdown as Pm-149 decays.
