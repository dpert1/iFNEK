NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Fallout Activity Decay Rule

\[R = R_1 t^{-1.2}\]

  • \(R\): dose rate at time \(t\) [rad/hr or R/hr]
  • \(R_1\): dose rate at 1 hour after detonation
  • \(t\): time after detonation [hours]

The "7-10 rule" approximation: for every 7-fold increase in time, dose rate decreases by a factor of 10. Fallout dose rates drop rapidly in the first hours, but long-lived isotopes can remain hazardous for days to weeks.
