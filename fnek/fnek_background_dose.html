NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Average Background Radiation Dose

~1 mrem/day (~360 mrem/year)

The U.S. average annual background dose is approximately 620 mrem/year when including medical exposures (~310 mrem/year from natural background alone). Sources include cosmic radiation, terrestrial radionuclides (radon, soil), internal radionuclides (K-40, C-14), and medical/occupational exposures.
