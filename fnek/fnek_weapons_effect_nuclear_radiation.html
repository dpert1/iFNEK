NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Weapons Effect: Nuclear Radiation

Nuclear radiation (initial and residual) accounts for approximately 15% of a nuclear weapon's total energy.

  • Initial (prompt) radiation: Neutrons and gamma rays emitted within the first minute. Range limited by atmospheric attenuation; significant within ~1–2 km for kiloton weapons.
  • Residual radiation (fallout): Radioactive fission products deposited downwind. Dose rate decays approximately as \(R = R_1 t^{-1.2}\). Primary hazard for large areas downwind over hours to days.
