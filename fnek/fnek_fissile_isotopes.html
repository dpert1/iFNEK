Common Fissile Isotopes

  • U-235: The only naturally occurring fissile nuclide (0.711 wt% in natural uranium).
  • Pu-239: Bred from U-238 via neutron capture; primary fissile material in plutonium weapons and MOX fuel.
  • U-233: Bred from Th-232; used in thorium fuel cycles.
  • Pu-241: Fissile; builds up in reactor fuel during operation.

Fissile nuclides sustain fission with thermal (slow) neutrons.
