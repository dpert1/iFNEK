NE 452 — Instrumentation and Shielding

Common Radiation Detector Types

  • Gas-filled: Ionizing radiation creates ion pairs in fill gas; collected by electrodes. Types include ionization chambers, proportional counters, and GM tubes. Robust and inexpensive.
  • Scintillation: Radiation excites a scintillator material (NaI, BGO, plastic) producing light photons detected by a PMT. Good for gamma spectroscopy and high count rates.
  • Semiconductor: Radiation creates electron-hole pairs in Si or Ge. Very low energy per pair → superior energy resolution. HPGe requires cryogenic cooling.
