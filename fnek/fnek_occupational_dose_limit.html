NE 474 — Radiological Safety

Annual Occupational Dose Limits

The annual limit for occupational radiation exposure to an adult is the more limiting of:

  • 0.05 Sv (5 rem) effective dose to the whole body
  • 0.5 Sv (50 rem) to any individual organ (except eyes)
  • 0.15 Sv (15 rem) to the lens of the eye

These limits apply to planned, controlled occupational exposures. Emergency dose limits are separate and higher. Members of the public are limited to 0.001 Sv (0.1 rem) per year from licensed facilities.
