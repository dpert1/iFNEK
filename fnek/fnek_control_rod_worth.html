NE 355 — Nuclear Reactor Engineering

Control Rod Worth Limit

Control rod worth must be less than $1.00 (one dollar of reactivity)

One dollar (\$1.00) of reactivity equals the delayed neutron fraction \(\beta\). If a single rod has worth ≥ \$1.00, its sudden ejection could drive the reactor prompt critical — a condition where the chain reaction is sustained by prompt neutrons alone, causing an uncontrollable power excursion.
