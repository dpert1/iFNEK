Charged Particle Interaction: Bremsstrahlung

Bremsstrahlung (German: "braking radiation") is electromagnetic radiation emitted when a charged particle is deflected by the Coulomb field of an atomic nucleus.

Significant primarily for electrons (light particles); negligible for heavy charged particles at diagnostic energies. Bremsstrahlung production scales as Z² of the absorber — high-Z materials produce more. This is the basis for X-ray tube operation.
