Good Moderator Properties

  1. Low atomic mass number (A): More energy transferred per elastic collision — lighter nuclei are more effective at slowing neutrons.
  2. Low microscopic absorption cross section (σ_a): Moderator should not parasitically absorb neutrons needed for the chain reaction.
  3. High microscopic scattering cross section (σ_s): High probability of scattering interactions ensures neutrons slow down efficiently.
