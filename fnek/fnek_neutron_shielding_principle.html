NE 452 — Instrumentation and Shielding

Basic Principle of Neutron Shielding

Two-step process: slow down fast neutrons, then absorb thermal neutrons.

  1. Moderation: Use low-Z material (water, polyethylene, concrete) to thermalize fast neutrons via elastic scattering.
  2. Absorption: Use high-absorption material (borated polyethylene, cadmium, B₄C) to capture the now-thermal neutrons before they escape.

Note: high-Z gamma shields are poor neutron shields, and vice versa — composite shields address both radiation types in layered designs.
