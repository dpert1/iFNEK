Figure 3: Chart of the Nuclides — Nuclear Process Locations

Shape:

Grid with the original nucleus at center. Surrounding cells show reaction products by particle in/out. Axes represent changes in proton (Z) and neutron (N) number.

Significance:

  • Each nuclear reaction moves a nuclide in a specific direction on the chart of the nuclides.
  • \((n, \gamma)\) capture: moves one step right (N+1, same Z).
  • \(\beta^-\) decay: moves diagonally up-left (Z+1, N-1).
  • \(\alpha\) decay: moves down-left (Z-2, N-2).
  • Understanding these directions is essential for tracing decay chains and transmutation in reactors.
