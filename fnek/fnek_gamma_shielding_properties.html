NE 452 — Instrumentation and Shielding

Good Gamma-Ray Shielding Properties

  • High atomic number (Z): High-Z materials have more electrons per atom, increasing photoelectric cross section (∝ Z⁴⁻⁵) and pair production (∝ Z²).
  • High linear attenuation coefficient (μ): Greater attenuation per unit thickness means thinner, more compact shields. Lead (Pb) is the most common gamma shield material due to high Z and density.
