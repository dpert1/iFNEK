Q-Value of a Nuclear Reaction

\[Q = -\Delta m c^2\]

The Q-value is the energy released (Q > 0) or absorbed (Q < 0) in a nuclear reaction. \(\Delta m\) is the mass difference between products and reactants. Calculated from atomic mass tables using \(1 \ \text{amu} = 931.494 \ \text{MeV}/c^2\).
