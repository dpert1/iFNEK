Figure 2: Fission Product Yield Curve

Shape:

Double-humped curve. Y-axis: fission yield (%), X-axis: mass number A (70–160). Two peaks near A ~ 90–100 and A ~ 130–140 with a valley near A ~ 118 (symmetric fission is rare).

Significance:

  • Fission is asymmetric — nuclei preferentially split into one light and one heavy fragment.
  • The 14 MeV (fast neutron) curve is slightly flatter/broader than the thermal curve — higher-energy fission allows more symmetric splits.
  • Peak products include Kr/Sr (light group) and Xe/Cs/Ba (heavy group) — these are the isotopes of primary concern for fallout and reactor fission products.
  • The valley at symmetric fission reflects nuclear shell structure effects.
