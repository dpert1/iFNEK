Gamma Interaction: Compton Scatter

The incident photon scatters off a loosely bound (essentially free) electron, transferring part of its energy. Both the scattered photon and recoil electron continue in new directions.

  • Dominant at intermediate energies (0.1–10 MeV)
  • Cross section scales linearly with Z (electrons per atom)
  • Scattered photon energy: \(E^\prime = \frac{E_\gamma}{1 + \frac{E_\gamma}{m_e c^2}(1-\cos\theta)}\)
