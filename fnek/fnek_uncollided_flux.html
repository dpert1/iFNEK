Uncollided Gamma Flux from a Point Source

\[\phi = \frac{S \, e^{-\mu r}}{4\pi r^2}\]

  • \(\phi\): uncollided flux [#/cm²-s]
  • \(S\): source strength [#/s]
  • \(\mu\): linear attenuation coefficient [cm⁻¹]
  • \(r\): distance from source [cm]

Combines inverse-square geometric spreading with exponential attenuation through material.
