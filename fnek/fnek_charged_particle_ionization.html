Charged Particle Interaction: Ionization

Ionization is the primary energy-loss mechanism for heavy charged particles (alphas, protons) and electrons in matter.

The incident particle transfers energy to bound atomic electrons, ejecting them from their atoms and creating ion pairs. Energy loss per unit path length is described by the Bethe-Bloch formula. Most of a charged particle's dose is deposited near the end of its range (Bragg peak).
