NE 450 — Nuclear Weapons Effects

Supercritical Assembly: Implosion-Type

Implosion-type assembly uses Pu-239 or U-235.

A subcritical fissile core (pit) is surrounded by precisely shaped conventional explosive lenses. Simultaneous detonation drives a symmetric inward shockwave that compresses the pit, increasing its density above the critical density threshold. Much faster than gun-type, allowing use of Pu-239 despite its high spontaneous fission rate. More compact and efficient. Used in the Fat Man weapon and all modern nuclear warheads.
